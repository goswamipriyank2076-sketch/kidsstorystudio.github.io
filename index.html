About the channel

Nanhi Nagariya is a Hindi channel for children aged 4 to 10 and their parents. Each video is a short animated story told in simple words, the way a grandparent would tell it, and ends with a moral lesson.

Kids can meet the same friends again and again in new adventures: Chhotu Chooza, Gopi Bakri, Bunty Bhalu and Riya Khargosh.

Moral stories

Short naitik kahaniyan about honesty, sharing, kindness and courage.

Gyan & fun facts

Easy, true facts about animals, nature and the world around us.

Bal geet

Original rhymes and songs that kids can sing along to.

Longer stories

Bedtime-length stories and weekly collections of the week's shorts.

Safe for kids

  • No scary, violent or unsuitable content.
  • Simple Hindi that young children can understand.
  • Every story ends with a good lesson.

About this app (Kids Story Studio)

Kids Story Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of Nanhi Nagariya. It publishes the channel's own videos to the owner's own accounts:

  • YouTube, through the YouTube Data API
  • Instagram (Reels), through the Instagram Graph API

The app has no public users. It does not collect any data from viewers or anyone else. See the Privacy Policy for details.
