Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Kids Story Studio is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Nanhi Nagariya channel. It uploads the channel's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It has no other users.

What data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of viewers or anyone else.

Permissions used

  • YouTube: only youtube.upload, to upload videos to the owner's channel.
  • Instagram: only what is needed to publish Reels to the owner's account: instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish and pages_show_list.

Where access tokens are kept

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's private computer and in private GitHub secrets. Access can be removed at any time:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Meta: Facebook Settings → Business Integrations

Data deletion

The app stores no user data. For any question about data, email us at the address below and we will reply within 7 days.
